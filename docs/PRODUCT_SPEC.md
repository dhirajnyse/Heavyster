# Heavyster Product Spec

## Product Thesis

Heavyster is a global heavy equipment rental listing SaaS for rental companies.

Rental companies list equipment, manage availability, upload verification details, and receive direct enquiries. Heavyster does not collect rental payments in phase one.

## Primary Users

- Independent heavy equipment rental companies
- Regional rental yards
- Crane, earthmoving, access, compaction, and roadwork specialists
- Contractors searching for machines by category, location, and availability

## Phase 1 MVP

Supplier account:

- Company profile
- Branch/location details
- Contact routes: phone, email, WhatsApp, website
- Verification status

Equipment listing:

- Category and subcategory
- Make, model, year, capacity, attachments, operator option
- Photos
- Availability status
- Rental terms summary
- Region and service radius
- Document checklist

Marketplace:

- Search by equipment, region, category, availability
- Supplier card
- Equipment detail page
- Direct enquiry packet

Billing:

- USD 9 monthly per active listing
- USD 99 yearly per active listing
- Listing status: draft, active, paused, expired

## Phase 1 Non-Goals

- Heavyster does not collect rental payment
- Heavyster does not own equipment
- Heavyster does not guarantee delivery
- Heavyster does not provide insurance
- Heavyster does not mediate damage disputes

## Success Metrics

- Suppliers onboarded
- Active paid listings
- Verified listings
- Direct enquiries sent
- Supplier response time
- Listing renewal rate
- Enquiries per active listing
